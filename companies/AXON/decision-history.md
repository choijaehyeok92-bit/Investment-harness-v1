### Rationale

Axon's business quality and growth evidence are strong: Q2 revenue grew 35.3%, ARR grew 39%, NRR reached 126%, and future contracted bookings grew 41% to $15.1 billion. The integrated device/data/workflow ecosystem appears to be strengthening and newer markets provide meaningful long-duration optionality.

No position is initiated because per-share economics have not yet caught up with the quality narrative. First-half 2026 free cash flow was negative, SBC remains very high, shares outstanding continue to increase, acquisitions are consuming capital, and the current valuation requires exceptional growth duration and margin conversion.

### Unresolved underwriting questions

1. Does annual dilution normalize toward low-single digits or below as XSP and CEO award expense matures?
2. Does GAAP operating margin and owner-FCF margin inflect materially upward as software mix scales?
3. Do Dedrone, Prepared, Carbyne and other acquisitions earn acceptable incremental returns rather than merely expand reported TAM?
4. Does valuation create a sufficient expectation gap for a starter position?

### Next decision triggers

- Q3/Q4 ARR, NRR and future contracted bookings trends.
- Full-year SBC, diluted share count and ATM issuance.
- GAAP operating margin, operating cash flow and FCF conversion.
- International, enterprise, AI Era and Axon 911 conversion from bookings into revenue.
- Price falling to a level that creates at least ~20% discount to a re-underwritten base value without thesis deterioration.
