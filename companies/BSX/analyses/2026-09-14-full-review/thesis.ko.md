## Thesis
Boston Scientific is a high-quality minimally invasive medtech platform with durable physician relationships, clinical evidence, regulatory barriers and procedure-linked recurring demand. FARAPULSE PFA and WATCHMAN LAAC established genuine category leadership, while ICVT, neuromodulation and endoscopy diversify the installed franchise. At the Sep. 11 reference price of $42.98, valuation no longer embeds the extreme growth expectations that existed near the 52-week high.

The present investment question is therefore not whether BSX is a good company. It is whether the company can preserve cardiac moat quality and earn acceptable incremental returns on Penumbra and MiRus after deploying unusually large amounts of capital.

## Operating evidence
- FY2025 sales: **$20.074B**, +19.9% reported / +15.8% organic.
- Q2 2026 sales: **$5.442B**, +7.5% reported / +7.0% organic.
- H1 2026 sales: **$10.646B**, +9.5% reported / +8.1% organic.
- Q2/H1 gross margin expanded materially, aided by higher-margin mix as well as some non-structural benefits.
- FARAPULSE remains a PFA leader, but U.S. competitive share loss is now visible.
- WATCHMAN remains the global LAAC leader, but referral/procedure dynamics weakened and management does not assume a 2027 recovery until evidence improves.

## Penumbra / MiRus capital-allocation issue
Penumbra consideration is approximately $15B equity value, around $11B cash and $4B BSX stock, with roughly 41M BSX shares fixed at signing. The FTC Second Request means regulatory closing risk remains unresolved in the latest primary evidence located.

A simplified first-full-year post-Penumbra normalization uses **$3.3B owner cash / ~1.490B shares = $2.21 per share**. At $42.98 that is approximately **19.4x** owner cash. This is an analyst estimate, not management guidance.

MiRus adds a second capital-allocation test: $1.6B already deployed for equity plus a TAVR option, with another $3B option price after clinical/regulatory milestones.

## Cyber incident
The August 25 incident disrupted manufacturing, order processing and shipping and made the July 29 FY2026 guidance unreliable. By September 9, manufacturing, order fulfillment and shipping were fully restored and product flow was at or above normal levels, but Q3/FY impact and final forensic/data consequences remain open. This is currently an operational risk rather than evidence of permanent product impairment.

## Why WATCH rather than STARTER
The valuation gap is now positive, but Harness policy puts Hard Veto ahead of score. Two unresolved investigations remain:

1. **Moat shrinkage:** Watchman utilization/referral weakness and PFA share loss must stabilize.
2. **Incremental ROIC collapse:** Penumbra and MiRus require evidence that premium acquisition prices can translate into owner-cash/share growth after financing.

The cyber event adds near-term uncertainty but is not, on current evidence, a standalone permanent-loss trigger.

## What changes the decision
Upgrade evidence: clean October 28 cyber quantification, stabilized PFA share, recovering Watchman trajectory, Penumbra clearance/financing with a credible deleveraging plan, and 6-8%+ normalized owner-cash/share compounding.

Downgrade evidence: persistent sub-5% organic growth, continuing PFA share loss, structural Watchman stagnation, sub-hurdle Penumbra/MiRus economics, or material lasting cyber/customer-data damage.

## Bottom line
**Business quality remains high and the valuation has become genuinely interesting. The stock is close enough to a value-oriented review zone to merit P1 attention, but the evidence threshold for a concentrated entry has not been met.** The next decisive checkpoint is the October 28, 2026 Q3 update.
