# AVGO Red Team — 2026-09-04

## Strongest bear case

The market may be extrapolating an unusually intense AI infrastructure build-out into a durable normal state. Broadcom's custom-accelerator revenue is concentrated among a small number of hyperscalers, so a single architecture change, delayed deployment, customer insourcing or competitive win by Marvell could create a step-function revenue miss rather than a gradual slowdown.

The same customer concentration that creates operating leverage also creates fragility. Broadcom disclosed that its top five end customers represented ~40% of FY2025 revenue. One semiconductor distributor represented 32% of revenue. If AI design wins are less diversified than consolidated revenue, true economic concentration may be higher than reported channel concentration suggests.

VMware is another potential fault line. Broadcom's monetization strategy can lift near-term software economics while reducing ecosystem breadth, partner goodwill or logo retention. If customers accelerate migration to public cloud, alternative virtualization or container-native infrastructure, current software margins could prove less durable than expected.

The balance sheet is robust in cash-generation terms but not pristine. Debt remains around the mid-$60B range following VMware. A simultaneous AI downturn and software attrition would reduce the margin of safety and compress the valuation multiple.

Finally, valuation still requires strong execution. At ~$357/share and about $1.7T equity value, a merely 'good' outcome is insufficient. The stock needs multi-year FCF compounding. If FCF growth falls toward the mid-teens while the market assigns a 20x multiple, permanent impairment from today's price is plausible even without operational failure.

## Thesis falsifiers

1. A top custom-accelerator customer materially reduces Broadcom content or shifts a major program to a competitor.
2. AI semiconductor revenue falls materially below management's FY2027 trajectory for reasons other than short timing shifts.
3. Ethernet AI networking share/attach declines while proprietary alternatives gain.
4. VMware renewal/retention deteriorates enough to cause structural infrastructure-software revenue decline.
5. FCF/share fails to grow despite revenue growth because of SBC, working capital, margin pressure or share dilution.
6. Customer concentration rises further rather than broadening across new AI customers.
7. The share price rerates to a level that requires near-perfect FY2028 execution before evidence improves.

## What the bull case may be missing

- Company AI revenue guidance is unusually large and may be more supply- and customer-committed than typical top-down AI forecasts.
- Broadcom's moat is not only custom compute; networking, optics and systems can increase content per AI cluster.
- Infrastructure software materially reduces cyclicality and can finance semiconductor R&D and debt reduction.
- A larger number of frontier-model customers could diversify the custom-silicon revenue base faster than current reported concentration suggests.

## Red-team verdict

The thesis survives red-team review, but **customer/design-win concentration remains the main unresolved Hard Veto investigation item**. Do not increase solely because the post-earnings stock price declined.
