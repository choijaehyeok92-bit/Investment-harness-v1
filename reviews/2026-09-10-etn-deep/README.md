## Why the business quality is high

Q2 2026 sales reached $8.531B with 14% organic growth. Electrical Americas grew 18% organically, produced a 27.5% operating margin and showed 41% rolling-order growth. Firm-commitment backlog was approximately $24.1B at June 30, and about 71% was targeted for delivery within twelve months. Electrical Global and Aerospace order/backlog indicators were also strong.

The structural thesis is broader than a single AI-data-center cycle: data centers, grid modernization, utility/industrial electrification, reshoring and aerospace all contribute. Eaton's product scope combines distribution, protection, power quality, modular systems, service and — after Boyd Thermal — liquid cooling from chip to grid.

## Why the score does not authorize a purchase

2026 changed Eaton's capital structure materially. H1 acquisition cash spending was approximately $11.079B, primarily Boyd Thermal ($9.549B) and Ultra PCS ($1.529B), and June debt rose to about $20.611B against only $0.695B of cash and short-term investments. The acquisitions were funded largely with new borrowings. Operating survival is not dependent on external capital, but acquisition ROIC and deleveraging are now central shareholder-value variables.

At the $408.35 reference close, the Base value of approximately $297.35 is about 27% below market price. Under retained Base margins and a 19x terminal multiple, the price requires roughly 11.55% revenue CAGR for years 2-10, above management's 6-9% organic revenue CAGR target through 2030. Current order growth means that path is not demonstrably impossible, so the valuation veto is INVESTIGATE rather than REJECT.

## Hard Veto summary

PASS: management/accounting integrity, external capital dependence, persistent dilution, low-quality growth, moat shrinkage, fatal concentration.

INVESTIGATE: incremental ROIC collapse, price requiring unrealistic bull case, permanent-loss probability.

No single customer exceeded 10% of Eaton net sales in 2025, so customer concentration is not considered fatal. Conversely, the combination of premium M&A, leverage and a valuation above Base creates meaningful permanent-loss risk even with low bankruptcy probability.

## Key increase evidence

A future buy authorization requires multiple evidence improvements, not merely a lower share price: post-integration Boyd/Ultra after-tax incremental ROIC above 9%; materially lower net debt; Electrical backlog conversion and OCF-PPE per share improving together; favorable Mobility RMT value transfer; and durable pricing/margins after competing industry capacity expands.
